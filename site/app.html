Kuwait NBCCNational Basic Cybersecurity Controls

Kuwait now has a mandatory cybersecurity floor.

Every entity under the mandate of the National Cyber Security Center has to meet 44 controls. This tool holds all of them, scores where you stand, and never sends a byte anywhere.

Every control, in full

The official minimum requirement of each control, broken into the individual statements an assessor would check one by one.

What to do, and when

Work is ranked by how much it unblocks, how early its phase falls, how much of it is still open, and how little it costs to start.

Work you have already done

Section 1 of the Annex aligns the baseline with CIS Controls v8.1 IG1 and the NIST Cybersecurity Framework. The ISO 27001 column is added here as a convenience for entities already certified.

Kuwait NBCC Toolkit

An open toolkit for Annex (1) to Decision No. 2 of 2026 of the National Cyber Security Center of the State of Kuwait. Built by Ali AlEnezi.

Not an official instrument

This is a readiness aid. The authoritative text is the Annex as published in Kuwait Al Youm issue 1785. Nothing here is a determination of compliance by NCSC.
